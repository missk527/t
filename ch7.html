Power and Energy – Revision Questions

  1. What is power?
    → The rate at which energy is transferred or work is done.
  2. Write the equation for power using energy.
    → Power = Energy transferred ÷ Time.
  3. Write the equation for power using work done.
    → Power = Work done ÷ Time.
  4. When is work done in physics?
    → When a force moves an object over a distance.
  5. Why is work done when a car is pushed along a road?
    → Because a force is applied to move the car a certain distance.
  6. Why is no work done when electrical energy is transferred through a wire?
    → Because no force or movement is involved.
  7. What is the unit of power?
    → Watts (W).
  8. What is the unit of energy?
    → Joules (J).
  9. What is the unit of work done?
    → Joules (J).
  10. What is the unit of time?
    → Seconds (s).
  11. How much energy is transferred by a 60 W device in 20 seconds?
    → 1,200 J.
  12. How much energy is transferred by a 50 W device in 30 seconds?
    → 1,500 J.
  13. How long does it take to transfer 1,200 J using 60 W?
    → 20 seconds.
  14. How long does it take to transfer 1,500 J using 50 W?
    → 30 seconds.
  15. What is the power if 1,200 J is transferred in 20 s?
    → 60 W.
  16. What is the power if 1,500 J is transferred in 30 s?
    → 50 W.
  17. Which lamp has the greater power output?
    → The left lamp.
  18. Why is no work done by a stationary electrical device?
    → Because no force or movement is involved.
  19. What information label is found on electrical appliances?
    → A label showing its power in watts.
  20. What does the power rating of a device tell us?
    → The rate at which it transfers energy.
  21. How long is 3 minutes in seconds?
    → 180 seconds.
  22. Convert 3 minutes to seconds.
    → 180 seconds.
  23. Write the equation to calculate energy transferred.
    → Energy transferred = Power × Time.
  24. How much energy is transferred by a 1,100 W kettle in 180 s?
    → 198,000 J.
  25. How do you convert joules to kilojoules?
    → Divide by 1,000.
  26. Convert 198,000 J into kilojoules.
    → 198 kJ.
  27. Convert 9,000 J into kilojoules.
    → 9 kJ.
  28. How long does it take to transfer 9,000 J at 450 W?
    → 20 seconds.
  29. How much energy is transferred at 450 W in 20 s?
    → 9,000 J.
  30. What is the power if 9,000 J is transferred in 20 s?
    → 450 W.
  31. What does “rate” mean in physics?
    → The amount of something happening per unit of time.
  32. Why is power called a rate?
    → Because it measures how quickly energy is transferred or work is done.
  33. What happens to power if the same energy is transferred in less time?
    → The power increases.
  34. Why does a higher power device transfer more energy each second?
    → Because it transfers more energy per second.
  35. Why does work involve both force and distance?
    → Because work is done when a force moves something through a distance.
  36. Which two quantities are needed to calculate power using energy?
    → Energy transferred and time.
  37. Which two quantities are needed to calculate power using work done?
    → Work done and time.
  38. Why rearrange the power equation?
    → To make energy transferred the subject.
  39. Why is a formula triangle useful?
    → It shows how variables relate and helps rearrange equations easily.
  40. What happens if time is not converted into seconds in calculations?
    → You would get the wrong energy value.
