Maintaining Biodiversity – Revision Questions

  1. What human activities reduce biodiversity?
    → Climate change, deforestation, and dumping waste.
  2. What are the effects of reduced biodiversity?
    → Reduced biodiversity and damaged ecosystems.
  3. What is meant by biodiversity?
    → The variety and number of species in an ecosystem.
  4. How can we protect ecosystems from human damage?
    → Reduce harmful practices, reduce their impact, and help ecosystems withstand them.
  5. Why are breeding programmes used?
    → To reduce extinction risk in endangered species.
  6. What is a breeding programme?
    → Breeding animals under controlled conditions rather than in the wild.
  7. Why are breeding programmes successful?
    → It increases population size safely.
  8. What may happen to animals bred in captivity?
    → They may be reintroduced to the wild.
  9. Why can reintroduction be difficult?
    → Because the natural habitat may still be unsafe.
  10. What is habitat protection?
    → Creating protected areas and regenerating habitats.
  11. Why is habitat protection important?
    → To keep habitats safe so organisms can survive and recover.
  12. Name two important coastal habitats.
    → Mangroves and coral reefs.
  13. Why is habitat regeneration important?
    → It restores habitats that support many species and ecosystem health.
  14. Why is farming both necessary and harmful to biodiversity?
    → We need it to produce food, but it can harm habitats and wildlife.
  15. How can farming be made more wildlife-friendly?
    → By introducing laws or paying farmers to adopt wildlife-friendly methods.
  16. What is a field margin?
    → A narrow strip around a field where wild plants can grow.
  17. What is a hedgerow?
    → A border of shrubs and trees around a field.
  18. How do field margins and hedgerows help biodiversity?
    → They provide extra habitats and food sources, supporting more species.
  19. What does limiting deforestation mean?
    → How much deforestation is allowed each year.
  20. Which greenhouse gas is most linked to climate change?
    → Carbon dioxide (CO₂).
  21. How can individuals reduce environmental damage?
    → Recycle waste and reduce unnecessary consumption.
  22. Why is recycling important?
    → It reduces waste produced and sent to landfill.
  23. How does recycling protect habitats?
    → More land remains undamaged.
  24. How does reducing waste protect ecosystems?
    → Fewer toxic chemicals leak into the environment.
  25. Are environmental problems solvable?
    → Yes, they are theoretically fixable.
  26. What is the main difficulty in solving environmental problems?
    → Money or cost.
  27. Why do some people ignore biodiversity loss?
    → They often overlook the benefits of high biodiversity.
  28. Why does modern life harm the environment?
    → Modern living can require activities that damage the environment.
  29. Why is land needed for farming?
    → To grow enough food for everyone.
  30. Why is mining harmful to ecosystems?
    → Mining can destroy habitats and cause pollution.
  31. Why is mining still carried out?
    → To access rare metals.
  32. What products require rare metals?
    → Phones and computers.
  33. Why do some people rely heavily on natural resources?
    → People may depend directly on natural resources to survive.
  34. Why is the environment important to humans?
    → It provides essentials like materials, fuel, and land for food.
  35. Why are forests cut down for housing?
    → To obtain building materials and space for housing.
  36. Why is wood collected in some countries?
    → To get firewood for cooking.
  37. Why is land cleared in tropical regions?
    → To clear land for planting crops.
  38. What does prevention mean in conservation?
    → Reducing harmful practices.
  39. What does mitigation mean in conservation?
    → Reducing impact and increasing ecosystem resilience.
  40. Why is protecting biodiversity difficult worldwide?
    → Because costs, lifestyle demands, and different economic needs create barriers.
