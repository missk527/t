Energy Answers

  1. Energy is never created or destroyed.
  2. No, it cannot.
  3. It means energy changes form or moves from one object/store to another.
  4. Work has been done on the object.
  5. In one of the object’s energy stores.

Types of Energy Stores

  1. Heat energy related to the temperature of an object.
  2. The movement or motion of an object.
  3. Its position in a gravitational field.
  4. A stretched spring.
  5. Chemical bonds.
  6. Magnetic energy.
  7. Electrostatic energy.
  8. Nuclear energy.
  9. Energy can move between stores.
  10. Mechanically, electrically, by heating, or by radiation (light/sound).

Systems

  1. A collection of matter being considered in physics.
  2. Between objects or between different forms of energy.
  3. A system that can exchange energy and matter with the outside world.
  4. Through interaction with the outside world.
  5. A system separate from the outside world.
  6. No, neither can enter or leave.
  7. Zero overall change (energy is conserved).

Kettle Example

  1. The plug socket.
  2. The heating element.
  3. Thermal energy store of the heating element.
  4. The heating element transfers energy to the water.
  5. The water’s thermal energy store.
  6. Work done.

Work Done

  1. Mechanical and electrical.
  2. Using a force to move an object.
  3. Kicking a ball into the air.
  4. Chemical energy store of your leg muscles.
  5. Kinetic energy store of the ball.
  6. Work done when current flows.
  7. The energy needed to overcome resistance in wires.

Train Example

  1. The brakes.
  2. Friction.
  3. Transfer of energy from kinetic to thermal.
  4. The kinetic energy store of the wheels.
  5. The thermal energy store of the surroundings.
