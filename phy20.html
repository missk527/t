Refraction and Dispersion – Revision Questions

  1. What is refraction?
    → The change in direction of light as it passes from one medium to another.
  2. When does refraction occur?
    → When light enters a different medium at an angle (not perpendicular).
  3. What is meant by a medium?
    → The material a wave travels through.
  4. Why does light change direction in different media?
    → Because materials have different densities, affecting wave speed.
  5. How does light travel in a denser material?
    → Light travels slower in a denser (more optically dense) material.
  6. What happens to light when it enters a denser medium at an angle?
    → It slows down and may bend.
  7. What happens if light enters a new medium at 90° to the surface?
    → It continues straight with no change in direction.
  8. What is the normal?
    → A line drawn perpendicular to the surface at the point of incidence.
  9. How does light bend when entering a denser medium?
    → It bends towards the normal.
  10. How does light bend when entering a less dense medium?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  11. What is the incident ray?
    → The incoming ray that hits the boundary.
  12. What is the refracted ray?
    → The ray inside the second medium after bending.
  13. What is the emergent ray?
    → The ray that exits back into the original medium.
  14. What is the point of incidence?
    → The point where the ray hits the boundary.
  15. What is the angle of incidence?
    → The angle between the incident ray and the normal.
  16. What is the angle of refraction?
    → The angle between the refracted ray and the normal.
  17. What line is used as a reference for measuring angles?
    → The normal.
  18. What is the first step when drawing a refraction diagram?
    → Draw the normal at the point of incidence.
  19. What do you do with the ray inside the glass block?
    → Continue it to the other side of the glass block.
  20. What must be drawn at the exit surface?
    → A new normal.
  21. What happens to light speed when it leaves a dense medium?
    → It speeds up.
  22. How does light bend when it leaves a dense medium?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  23. What equation links wave speed, frequency, and wavelength?
    → v = f × λ.
  24. What happens to frequency during refraction?
    → Frequency stays the same.
  25. What happens to wavelength when light slows down?
    → Wavelength decreases.
  26. What happens to wavelength when light speeds up?
    → Wavelength increases.
  27. Why does white light spread out in a prism?
    → Because different wavelengths refract by different amounts.
  28. What is dispersion?
    → The splitting of white light into colours.
  29. Define dispersion.
    → The spreading of white light into a spectrum of colours.
  30. Why does dispersion occur?
    → Because refraction depends on wavelength.
  31. What is white light made of?
    → Many different wavelengths of visible light.
  32. What is the band of colours called after dispersion?
    → A spectrum.
  33. Why is the normal important in ray diagrams?
    → It shows the reference direction for measuring bending and angles.
  34. Which two angles are measured in refraction?
    → Angle of incidence and angle of refraction.
  35. What happens when light enters glass from air?
    → It bends towards the normal.
  36. What happens when light leaves glass into air?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  37. What matters most in drawing refraction diagrams?
    → Correct bending direction matters more than the exact angle.
  38. What happens to light entering a denser medium?
    → It slows down and bends towards the normal.
  39. What happens to light entering a less dense medium?
    → It speeds up and bends away from the normal.
  40. Why does a prism produce a spectrum?
    → Different wavelengths refract differently, spreading into a spectrum.
