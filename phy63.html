Refraction and Dispersion – Physics Revision

  1. What is meant by refraction?
    → A change in direction of light when it moves between different media.
  2. When does refraction happen?
    → When light enters a new medium at an angle other than 90°.
  3. What is a medium in physics?
    → The material through which a wave travels.
  4. Why does light bend at a boundary?
    → Because wave speed changes in materials of different densities.
  5. How does light travel in optically denser materials?
    → It travels more slowly.
  6. What happens to light entering a denser medium?
    → It slows down and changes direction.
  7. What happens if light hits the surface straight on?
    → It continues without bending.
  8. What is the normal line?
    → A line drawn at right angles to the surface.
  9. How does light bend when entering glass from air?
    → It bends towards the normal.
  10. How does light bend when leaving glass into air?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  11. What is an incident ray?
    → The ray that approaches the boundary.
  12. What is a refracted ray?
    → The ray inside the second medium.
  13. What is an emergent ray?
    → The ray that leaves the material.
  14. What is the point of incidence?
    → Where the ray meets the surface.
  15. Define angle of incidence.
    → The angle between the incident ray and the normal.
  16. Define angle of refraction.
    → The angle between the refracted ray and the normal.
  17. Which line is used to measure angles?
    → The normal.
  18. What should be drawn first in a ray diagram?
    → The normal at the point of incidence.
  19. What happens to the ray inside a glass block?
    → It continues to the opposite face.
  20. What must be drawn at the exit point?
    → Another normal line.
  21. What happens to speed when light leaves a dense medium?
    → It increases.
  22. How does light bend when speed increases?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  23. What equation links speed, frequency, and wavelength?
    → v = f × λ.
  24. What stays constant when light refracts?
    → Frequency.
  25. What happens to wavelength when speed decreases?
    → It becomes shorter.
  26. What happens to wavelength when speed increases?
    → It becomes longer.
  27. Why does a prism separate colours?
    → Different wavelengths bend by different amounts.
  28. What is meant by dispersion?
    → The splitting of white light into colours.
  29. Define dispersion fully.
    → The spreading of white light into a spectrum.
  30. Why does refraction depend on wavelength?
    → Because each wavelength travels at a slightly different speed.
  31. What is white light made of?
    → Many wavelengths of visible light.
  32. What do we call the band of colours formed?
    → A spectrum.
  33. Why is the normal important?
    → It provides a reference for measuring angles.
  34. Which two angles are important in refraction?
    → Angle of incidence and angle of refraction.
  35. What happens when light goes from air to glass?
    → It bends towards the normal.
  36. What happens when light goes from glass to air?
    → It bends away from the normal.
  37. What is most important when drawing ray diagrams?
    → Correct bending direction.
  38. Describe light entering a denser material.
    → It slows down and bends towards the normal.
  39. Describe light entering a less dense material.
    → It speeds up and bends away from the normal.
  40. Why does a prism form a spectrum?
    → Different wavelengths refract by different amounts.
