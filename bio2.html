Units, resolution, and magnification — from metres to nanometres.

Metres → millimetres → micrometres → nanometres: each step multiplies the numerical value by 1,000. Going back one step means dividing by 1,000. Centimetres are a separate factor of 100 from metres.

02 / Seeing small objects

The naked eye

Seeing with only the eyes, without equipment.

Very small objects may require a microscope because the eye lacks sufficient resolution.

Resolution

The ability to distinguish two separate points.

The resolution limit is the shortest distance between two points that can still be seen as separate.

03 / Light and electron microscopes

Light microscopeElectron microscope
Typical resolution limit in these notes0.2 µm0.1 nm
What limits resolution?Wavelength of lightWavelength of electrons

0.2 µm = 200 nm. A limit of 0.1 nm is about 2,000 times smaller. Electrons have shorter wavelengths, allowing finer detail to be resolved. Resolution varies with the instrument.

04 / Magnification

Magnification means enlarging the appearance of an object. The object is the specimen; the image is its enlarged representation.

Magnification = Image size ÷ Object size

Use the same units for the image and object sizes to make the ratio accurate.

Worked example

Image size = 0.03 m
Object size = 0.00003 m

0.03 ÷ 0.00003 = 1,000×

Useful magnification is limited by resolution. Increasing magnification without enough resolution produces a bigger, blurrier image without revealing more detail.

05 / Quick recall

Open each question to reveal the answer.

How many metres are in one kilometre?

1,000 m, or 103 m.

What is one micrometre in metres?

10−6 m.

What does resolution mean?

The ability to distinguish two separate points.

Why can electron microscopes resolve finer detail?

Electrons have shorter wavelengths than visible light.

What is the magnification formula?

Magnification = image size ÷ object size, using the same units.

Does making an image bigger always reveal more detail?

No. The amount of detail is limited by resolution.
