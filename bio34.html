Photosynthesis – Revision Questions

  1. Why do plants need to photosynthesise?
    → To grow and survive.
  2. Where in the plant does photosynthesis mainly take place?
    → In the leaves.
  3. Which organelles carry out photosynthesis?
    → Chloroplasts.
  4. What green pigment absorbs light energy?
    → Chlorophyll.
  5. What type of energy is needed for photosynthesis?
    → Light energy.
  6. What raw materials are needed for photosynthesis?
    → Carbon dioxide and water.
  7. What products are made in photosynthesis?
    → Glucose and oxygen.
  8. Write the word equation for photosynthesis.
    → Carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (with light).
  9. Write the symbol equation for photosynthesis.
    → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (with light).
  10. Why is light needed for photosynthesis?
    → Because light energy is required for the reaction.
  11. What type of reaction is photosynthesis?
    → Endothermic.
  12. What does “endothermic” mean?
    → It uses energy from the surroundings.
  13. How does light supply energy to the plant?
    → By light transferring energy to chloroplasts.
  14. How does carbon dioxide enter the leaf?
    → By diffusion.
  15. Through which structures does carbon dioxide enter?
    → Through stomata.
  16. Where does water come from?
    → From the soil.
  17. Which tissue transports water in plants?
    → Xylem.
  18. Which substance is made in photosynthesis and used by the plant?
    → Glucose.
  19. Why is oxygen not stored by plants?
    → There is plenty of oxygen in the air and it is mainly a by-product.
  20. What process uses glucose and oxygen to release energy?
    → Respiration.
  21. What process supplies energy for plant growth?
    → Respiration.
  22. What object stores energy in a similar way to glucose in plants?
    → A battery.
  23. Where does the original energy for photosynthesis come from?
    → The sun’s light energy.
  24. What substance is made from glucose to build cell walls?
    → Cellulose.
  25. What substance is glucose often stored as in plants?
    → Starch.
  26. Why is starch good for storage in plants?
    → It is a large polymer so more glucose can be stored in less space.
  27. Why does starch not affect water balance in cells?
    → It won’t draw water into the cell by osmosis.
  28. What else is made from glucose besides carbohydrates?
    → Water.
  29. When do plants mainly use stored starch?
    → At night or in winter when photosynthesis is low.
  30. Which sugar is transported around the plant?
    → Glucose.
  31. Which mineral ions are needed to make proteins?
    → Nitrate ions.
  32. Where do plants get mineral ions from?
    → The soil.
  33. What are proteins needed for in plants?
    → Proteins.
  34. What food stores contain a lot of energy in plants?
    → Oils and fats.
  35. Where are food stores commonly found in plants?
    → Seeds.
  36. Why is photosynthesis important to the plant?
    → It provides energy and materials for building and storage.
  37. What is the name of the process that makes glucose in plants?
    → Photosynthesis.
  38. Which factor affects the rate of photosynthesis most directly?
    → Light intensity.
  39. Name another factor that affects the rate of photosynthesis.
    → Temperature or carbon dioxide concentration.
  40. What are the remaining limiting factors of photosynthesis?
    → Carbon dioxide concentration and amount of chlorophyll.
