Esters and Esterification – Revision Questions

  1. What is an ester?
    → An organic compound with an ester functional group.
  2. Where is the ester functional group found in a molecule?
    → Somewhere in the middle of the molecule, not just at the end.
  3. What is the formula of the ester functional group?
    → –COO–.
  4. Which group is part of the ester functional group?
    → A carbonyl (C=O) group.
  5. What type of smell do esters usually have?
    → Sweet or fruity.
  6. Why do esters evaporate easily?
    → They are volatile.
  7. Why are esters used in perfumes?
    → Because they smell pleasant and evaporate easily.
  8. Why are esters used as flavourings?
    → Because they give sweet and fruity flavours and aromas.
  9. Which two substances react to make an ester?
    → A carboxylic acid and an alcohol.
  10. What is the name of the reaction that forms esters?
    → Esterification.
  11. Why is a catalyst used in esterification?
    → To speed up the reaction.
  12. Which catalyst is commonly used in esterification?
    → Concentrated sulfuric acid.
  13. Give the name of an example ester.
    → Ethyl ethanoate.
  14. Which acid forms ethyl ethanoate?
    → Ethanoic acid.
  15. Which alcohol forms ethyl ethanoate?
    → Ethanol.
  16. Which functional group is found in alcohols?
    → An –OH group.
  17. Which atom is lost from the alcohol during esterification?
    → A hydrogen (H) from its –OH group.
  18. What other product is formed during esterification?
    → Water.
  19. Which atoms combine to form water in esterification?
    → The –OH from the acid and the H from the alcohol.
  20. What is a by-product?
    → A product formed alongside the main product.
  21. What is the bond formed in an ester called?
    → The ester link.
  22. Why is it called a linking group?
    → Because it links the carboxylic acid part and the alcohol part.
  23. What is a carboxylic acid?
    → An organic acid containing –COOH.
  24. What type of compound contains an –OH group?
    → An alcohol.
  25. What is a functional group?
    → A group of atoms that determines chemical properties and reactions.
  26. Which part of the ester group comes from the alcohol?
    → The –O– part of –COO–.
  27. Which part of the ester group comes from the acid?
    → The C=O part.
  28. What property describes how easily a substance evaporates?
    → Volatility.
  29. Which substance gives the fruity smell in esterification products?
    → The ester.
  30. What is the chemical formula of water?
    → H₂O.
  31. What is the functional group of carboxylic acids?
    → –COOH.
  32. What is the functional group of esters?
    → –COO–.
  33. What causes the fragrance of many fruits?
    → The characteristic fragrance of many esters.
  34. What is the typical smell of esters?
    → Sweet or fruity.
  35. What effect does a catalyst have on a reaction?
    → It increases (speeds up) the reaction rate.
  36. Is a catalyst used up in a reaction?
    → No, a catalyst is not used up.
  37. What type of reaction is esterification?
    → A condensation reaction where water is formed.
  38. Which two fragments join together to form an ester?
    → The carboxylic acid fragment and the alcohol fragment.
  39. Give one use of esters.
    → Perfume.
  40. Give another use of esters.
    → Food flavourings.
