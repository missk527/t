Biology revision answers

  1. Cells are classified into two broad categories as: prokaryotic cells and eukaryotic cells.
  2. Eukaryotic cells are more complex because they have a nucleus and membrane-bound organelles.
  3. “Unicellular” means the organism consists of only one cell.
  4. The nucleus contains DNA (genetic material) and controls cell activities.
  5. The role of mitochondria is aerobic respiration and energy (ATP) production.
  6. The vacuole is important for plants because it maintains turgor pressure, helping keep the cell rigid and supported.
  7. No, bacterial cells do not have mitochondria.
  8. Kilometers in powers of 10: 1 km = 10³ meters.
  9. Resolution in microscopy is the ability to distinguish two close points as separate.
  10. The process of becoming a specialized cell is called cell differentiation.
  11. The main function of sperm cells is to fertilize the egg by delivering male DNA.
  12. The role of muscle cells is to contract and cause movement.
  13. Therapeutic cloning is producing stem cells genetically identical to a patient for treatment.
  14. The three main stages of the cell cycle are: interphase, mitosis, and cytokinesis.
  15. When the cell is not dividing, DNA is usually found as chromatin (long, uncoiled strands).
  16. The two copies of each chromosome in eukaryotic cells come from one parent each — one maternal and one paternal copy.
  17. Two small molecules that can diffuse through membranes are: oxygen (O₂) and carbon dioxide (CO₂).
  18. Cells need oxygen and glucose for respiration to release energy.
  19. A membrane that only allows certain molecules to pass through is called selectively permeable (partially permeable).
  20. Diffusion across a cell membrane is random, though it results in a net movement down a concentration gradient.
  21. The role of the cell membrane in diffusion is to control what enters and leaves the cell.
  22. During osmosis, water molecules move from a region of high water concentration (dilute solution) to low water concentration (more concentrated solution).
  23. Osmosis would stop between the two beakers when equilibrium is reached and water potential is equal on both sides.
  24. The cytoplasm is the jelly-like substance where many chemical reactions occur.
  25. Another simple way to describe osmosis is the movement of water across a partially permeable membrane.
  26. Osmosis stops in any system when there is no difference in water potential, meaning equilibrium has been reached.
