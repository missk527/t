The Cardiovascular System – Revision Questions

  1. What organs make up the cardiovascular system?
    → The heart and blood vessels.
  2. What type of diseases affect the heart and blood vessels?
    → Diseases of the cardiovascular system.
  3. Give examples of cardiovascular diseases.
    → Coronary heart disease, heart attacks, faulty valves, heart failure.
  4. What happens in coronary heart disease?
    → Coronary arteries become blocked by fatty material.
  5. What happens to the artery when fatty material builds up?
    → It becomes narrower.
  6. What effect does narrowing have on blood flow and oxygen supply?
    → Less blood flows through, so less oxygen reaches the heart muscle.
  7. Why does the heart need a good oxygen supply?
    → To pump blood effectively and keep working.
  8. What can happen if the heart muscle is starved of oxygen?
    → A heart attack.
  9. Name two treatments for blocked coronary arteries.
    → Stents and statins.
  10. What is a stent?
    → An expandable tube placed in arteries to keep them open.

Stents

  1. What is the purpose of a stent?
    → To keep arteries open and maintain blood flow.
  2. How long does stent surgery usually take?
    → The surgery is relatively quick.
  3. How long are stents effective for?
    → They are effective for a long time.
  4. What risks are involved in stent surgery?
    → Infection or heart attack.
  5. What complication can form near a stent?
    → A blood clot forming near the stent.

Statins

  1. What are statins?
    → A medication that alters cholesterol balance in the blood.
  2. What is cholesterol?
    → A type of lipid needed for health.
  3. Name the two main types of cholesterol.
    → LDL (bad) and HDL (good).
  4. Which type of cholesterol is harmful?
    → LDL.
  5. Which type of cholesterol is protective?
    → HDL.
  6. How do statins help prevent heart disease?
    → They lower LDL and increase HDL, reducing fatty deposits in arteries.
  7. How often must statins be taken?
    → They must be taken regularly for years.
  8. Why must statins be taken long-term?
    → Because cholesterol balance needs to be controlled long-term.
  9. Give one serious side effect of statins.
    → Kidney failure (also headaches and other side effects possible).
  10. What conditions do statins help prevent?
    → Strokes and heart attacks.

Heart Valves

  1. What is the function of heart valves?
    → To allow blood to flow in one direction only.
  2. What problems can occur with heart valves?
    → They can become stiff or fail to close properly.
  3. What happens if valves become stiff?
    → Not enough blood passes through.
  4. What happens if valves fail to close properly?
    → Blood leaks backwards.
  5. What types of replacement valves are available?
    → Biological valves or mechanical valves.
  6. Where do biological valves usually come from?
    → Pigs or cows.
  7. What risk is associated with valve replacement surgery?
    → Both require surgery and have a risk of blood clots.

Heart Failure and Replacement

  1. What is heart failure?
    → When the heart cannot pump blood properly around the body.
  2. What treatment may be needed for severe heart failure?
    → A new heart (heart transplant).
  3. What materials are artificial hearts made from?
    → Metals and plastic.
  4. Why are artificial hearts not permanent solutions?
    → Because they are not living tissue.
  5. Why are artificial hearts usually temporary?
    → They are less effective than real hearts and usually temporary until a donor is found.
  6. Why can animal hearts not be used for human transplants?
    → They are not compatible for human transplants.
  7. Why are heart transplants difficult to arrange?
    → Donor hearts are rare and hard to find.
  8. What risk follows a heart transplant?
    → The body’s immune system may reject the new heart.
